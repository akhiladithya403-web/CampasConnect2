Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does Roll Number login work?

You can sign in directly using your college Roll Number (e.g. 22CS108). All tokens, passes, and complaint receipts will be mapped to your Roll Number.

Q: How does Lost & Found match notification work?

When you post an item, our algorithm compares title, category, and location. If a match is found, an automated email is sent to your mailbox!

Q: How do Canteen Queue tokens work?

You generate a digital token without waiting in line. When your food is ready, you will hear an audio chime and get an order-ready reminder!
